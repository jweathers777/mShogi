# MSH-158 (Chess Decision Corrections): isolated pawn-race heuristic review

Verdict: no source-level blocker to bounded experimental evaluation. Production retention remains contingent on controlled decision evidence and wider regressions. Compared current chess_conventional_eval.rs against /tmp/msh158/eval-before-race.rs. No engines, builds, tests or held-out inspection performed by reviewer.

This is a general pawn-only heuristic: no fixture identity, opening, material exception or desired move is encoded. Relative ranks, side-to-move and initial double pushes determine optimistic promotion plies. The opposing minimum includes every pawn even when blocked, deliberately rejecting some genuine favorable races. A candidate must be strictly passed, have every runway square unoccupied and outside current enemy pawn attacks, and beat a generous enemy-king interception calculation at every landing. A lead of at least three plies is necessary. Multiple qualifying pawns set a per-side300 bonus rather than accumulate it. Only the endgame term changes; specialized KPK still determines its final score. The revised documented per-color bound remains below30000.

The existing targeted test covers color reflection, one-ply/equal races, faster opponent, obstruction, king interception, non-pawn material exclusion, enemy pawn exclusion, initial double-push timing, and KPK specialization. Add a multiple-qualifying-passer case to guard the300 cap; wider existing score-bound/mirror tests should be reused.

Limitations must remain explicit. This does not prove uninterrupted legal promotion: an existing pawn check or future forcing checks may require king detours. A three-ply lead does not prove that the resulting queen stops a distant passer or wins the queen ending. Current attack geometry and optimistic king distance are deliberately simple and may reject escorted winning races. Conversely, the score jump when a runway clears is substantial and may favor a false conversion; development controls and opponent-continuation evidence must decide whether it helps. These are ordinary limitations of a bounded classical heuristic, not reasons to demand a complete endgame solver before an experiment.

No native move, history, outcome or claim authority is modified. Root-trial evidence was not yet available for this review, so no decision-improvement claim is made here.
