SharedResult::Failure is sufficient for unsupported Xiangqi; reject Chess/Shogi result families on Xiangqi. Semantic undo/restoration may equal old records but must not revive bridge's monotonically retired request/process identities.

Preserve this unchanged where possible and test cross-game replacement/cancellation after completion as well as before launch.
