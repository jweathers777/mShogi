# MSH-159 (Classical Decision Evaluation): evaluator system review

Read-only source review of `crates/mshogi-search/src/chess_conventional_eval.rs`, the three named development roots and the MSH-158 report. No engine runs, builds, production edits or held-out inspection. This establishes implemented/missing mechanisms, not that a proposed coefficient or move is correct.

## Concrete diagnosis candidates

**0UOyM: no target-aware king activity in the relevant material.** After exf3+ Kxf3, both sides have lost one pawn, White's king has become more central, and Black has yet to realize the opened route. The evaluator rewards that White centrality immediately. It has no general bonus for Black's route toward the g3 pawn or the resulting distant race until static attacks/passers appear. Thus this route is largely a search-horizon responsibility in the current design. This is a concrete representational omission, not proof that adding a target bonus improves the decision. The MSH-158 g7g6 endpoint with White promoting first must remain a negative control: rewarding Black merely for a visually attractive route would be wrong there.

**0m5mI: promotion control is weakly represented compared with advance.** Along the published Kc3 …f4 exf4 …e3 Bc1 …e2 Bd2 line, the black e2 passer has advance5:185 endgame passer cp before escort and runway adjustments. Its e1 front square is empty, so the occupied-blocker halving does not apply. White's bishop on d2 controls promotion, but that only invokes the general quarter unsafe-path deduction (46cp); roughly139cp of rank bonus remains, plus50cp pawn-placement advance and pawn material. The static system does not encode the bishop's blockade role or Black king's inability to dislodge it on demand. Conversely White's distant a-pawn can have its whole runway marked unsafe by one current bishop ray, even if king penetration will remove that control. These are concrete feature/scale interactions worth tracing at identical known endpoints, not a proven wrong WDL or justification for a fixed tactical bonus.

**28pJK is a retention control, not a target to make more negative/positive.** The recent search fix selects Qc8. A large reference score gap does not identify an evaluator defect. Its tactical/checking consequences and actual root decision should survive endgame-focused changes. Do not enlarge king-danger coefficients merely to match its reference score.

## Plausible bounded experiments, in order

1. **Promotion-control-aware passer value.** Hypothesis: advanced pawns retain too much rank value when the block/promotion square is securely controlled by an opposing minor/king and the pawn cannot safely advance. Distinguish immediate occupied blockade, controlled next square, and farther controlled promotion; avoid calling any geometric control permanent. Modify the existing passer contribution rather than layering a second unrelated bonus. Inspect 0m5mI reference/native intermediate branches, plus clear protected passers, removable/hanging blockaders, same/opposite bishops, king-supported breakthroughs and exact KPK controls. Require correct alternative ranking and five-second decisions; reject a change that merely selects Kc3 while still retreating/valuing the blockade incorrectly.

2. **Pawn-ending king targets rather than bare centrality.** Hypothesis: king proximity to relevant pawn targets/front squares should complement centrality in low nonpawn material, particularly where a pawn exchange opens a route. Use a bounded, symmetric distance/target feature with blocked routes and opposing king/pawn control as uncertainty, not a fabricated shortest legal race proof. Compare actual0UOyM intermediate branches and distant breakthrough controls, including the known White-first-promotion endpoint. A simple Chebyshev race bonus already failed in MSH-158; do not repeat it under a different name. If route feasibility cannot be captured cheaply without a hidden search, report coverage as the remaining issue instead.

3. **Bishop restriction/coordination only if decomposition supports it.** A general own-pawn-color/blocked-pawn interaction or blockade-duty adjustment is conventional, but adding it before confirming which bishop is over/undervalued risks penalizing the very useful blockader. Use near-neighbor controls that change the relevant pawn color/control while preserving material, not a parameter sweep. Keep its effect separately identifiable from passer changes.

Do not start with blanket opposite-bishop draw scaling or a new special-case endgame recognizer: both roots are outside a proven exact material result, and shrinking scores does not establish better decisions. All candidate terms remain advisory; native histories, outcomes, claims and search are unchanged.

## Primary implementation precedents

[Stockfish11 classical evaluation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/sf_11/src/evaluate.cpp) weights passer king proximity by rank, distinguishes control of the block square versus the full promotion path, and includes bishop/pawn-color interaction. Its initiative code considers king infiltration, outflanking and pawns on both flanks. These support testing related general features, not copying its coefficients or treating its judgments as rules.

[Ethereal classical evaluation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AndyGrant/Ethereal/master/src/evaluate.c) separates passer rank, ability to advance, safety, king distances and uncontested promotion path. This is a useful structural contrast to one largely rank-driven bonus plus a quarter-path deduction. The referenced implementations carry GPL notices; this proposal uses conceptual descriptions, no copied code/tables. Source scores are not calibration targets.

## Evidence discipline

Freeze the actual full-history intermediate states and root-side perspective before comparing terms. Use existing native evaluation decomposition; first check specialized override and phase, then material, placement, pawn and mobility differences. Compare equivalent stages/remaining plans, not arbitrary equally long PV fragments. Preserve whether each endpoint was actually evaluated in the original search, returned through a bound, or only supplied for static diagnosis. Retain a candidate only with supported static ranking/decision benefit and wider development controls; final held-out data remain sealed until freeze.
