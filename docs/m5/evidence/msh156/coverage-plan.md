# Workstream B: bounded coverage and classical-judgment diagnosis

Read-only audit, 2026-11-10, repository fb87ca2 / production d53f344. No production edits, engine runs or held-out data access. This is a hypothesis shortlist, not a finding that any proposed algorithm is defective. It uses existing source plus MSH-154 (Chess Adversarial Assessment), MSH-155 (Chess Time Scaling) and MSH-152 (Classical Tactical Forecasting). The broader independently sourced benchmark must be frozen before candidate evaluation.

## Established facts and contrary evidence

The historical Nf5/Rf6 mistakes improve by the earliest successful sampled five-second allowance, while two/three-second samples remain weak. That local result implicates available useful search without distinguishing native cost, selective coverage and endpoint judgment. Fresh exact controls already pass at one second, so they cannot establish general improvement. Main first-move cutoff fractions are high in the MSH-154 sample; blanket ordering failure is unsupported. Previous MSH-152 traces established one PV LMR failure and repaired it; repeating its motivating roots is regression evidence, not transfer.

No new definite correctness defect was established by this source audit. The leading opportunities below require decision-level evidence before production changes.

## Ranked hypotheses and existing discriminators

1. **Selective fail-low acceptance still misses quiet defenses.** `crates/mshogi-search/src/chess_conventional.rs:1094` chooses LMR candidates; `:1174` leaves one-ply reductions on depth>=6/move>=8 PV alternatives and ordinary 1/2-ply non-PV reductions. `:1212` only verifies reduced moves exceeding alpha; a false fail-low remains unverified. Quiet omission gates at `:1115`–`:1155` can independently suppress late quiet defenses at depths<=2. This is conventional selectivity, not itself a bug. On new development failures compare default, `disable_lmr`, and `disable_quiet_omissions` independently, retaining the identical q frontier. Trace the actual omitted/reduced defense, its window, remaining depth and score; do not infer causality merely because a no-LMR PV contains it. Counterhypothesis: more complete coverage costs so much that equal-time quality is worse, or endpoints still misvalue the line. Broadly disabling reductions is not the default correction.

2. **Frontier recognizes a narrow forcing family while accepting stand-pat in other unstable positions.** `:1429` checks immediate queen-contact mates; `:1444` detects queen-contact threats; `:1456` permits stand-pat at all other non-check nodes before quiet checking moves; `:1476`–`:1506` considers quiet checks/contact-threat creation only at the first two q plies. `Q_PLIES=16`, `Q_FORCING_MIN=15`, `Q_DEFENCE_MIN=14` at `:28`–`:32`. A remote/discovered mate defense is verified only after the final contact-threat response (`:1329`). Quiet rook/minor mating threats, nonmating tactical threats, and later quiet forcing moves are not treated alike. This deliberately bounded policy avoids a second full-width search; the missing family is a hypothesis, not proof that indiscriminate expansion is beneficial. Select development failures with independently established forcing consequences and compare matched-horizon roots just before/after the frontier. If traces confirm omission, prototype one general admission guard, including counterexamples where apparent threats are legally irrelevant. Do not widen q globally based on the historical queen-related cases.

3. **Exchange/delta omissions can hide nonmaterial tactical compensation.** The legal least-attacker material sequence at `:553` is an estimate, not full exchange minimax; ordinary same-value attacker tie choices remain lexical. Main SEE omissions at `:1156` and q SEE/delta gates at `:1515`–`:1529` intentionally omit some sacrifices except checking, promotion, claim/outcome and unsettled guards. Compare `disable_see_pruning` and `disable_delta_pruning` separately. A quiet sacrifice that produces a later checking sequence is a plausible discriminator; merely negative SEE is not a defect. MSH-152 already repaired promotion-choice enumeration; preserve it. Require traced omitted forcing continuation, not reference-score disagreement alone.

4. **Classical geometric features can misvalue consequences even when continuation coverage is adequate.** `chess_conventional_eval.rs:412` builds dynamic terms; `:560`–`:616` estimates king-ring pressure and checking origins geometrically (explicitly leaves pins/discoveries to search); `:621` onward estimates threats from attacks/least-attacker values. These are heuristics, deliberately not legality or proof. Only elevate valuation after comparable matched-horizon searches actually admit the relevant defense, and independent quiet endpoint outcomes support a persistent ranking error. Existing `chess_frontier_profile` emits term decompositions for root and PV endpoint. Fresh-root reruns reset TT/order/extension context: label them supporting matched-horizon probes, not exact equivalence to an interior node. Keep MSH-153 (Classical Compensation Calibration) held absent this evidence; do not preselect king-safety/compensation bonuses.

## Small diagnostic sequence

- Freeze public development/held-out families and expectations first. Baseline all development roots at primary 5s, with a stratified 1s subset; do not let candidate answers influence source selection. Keep the historical eight-root set separate as regression material.
- From development only, select at most eight failures spanning tactical, defensive, strategic and endgame categories by frozen authority/severity criteria. Include at least two successful controls. This is the bounded causal panel, not the final quality sample.
- Reuse `crates/mshogi-search/examples/chess_frontier_profile.rs`: it already accepts complete histories, native root checks, per-root time/depth, all five single-factor ablations, bounded TT, and emits decisions, score/PV, completed/interrupted iterations, native work and static terms. No new framework needed.
- For relevant factors only, first compare fixed completed depths from the failing baseline, then equal 5s. Default + five ablations on eight roots is at most 240 nominal seconds at 5s if all are justified; prefer fewer factors from actual root mechanisms. Add focused 15s only where it changes attribution. Charge all failures and work to the round's ledger; do not silently treat a new task as a fresh allowance.
- Add bounded trace instrumentation only when an ablation changes a supported choice. Record actual reduced/omitted move, parent bound/window, relevant native history, remaining horizon, endpoint and legal best-defense sequence. Root move restrictions are not presently exposed by the frontier example; adding a diagnostic root-choice control is a specific missing capability if paired candidate analysis requires it, not a new framework.
- Stop an attribution branch when no relevant factor discriminates at practical time, or when a traced mechanism has sufficient controls to justify one coherent correction. Avoid a parameter grid. Exact SearchMode is unsuitable as a pruning-only control because it also removes quiescence/extensions.

## Candidate and round gates

Preserve a frozen predecessor binary/hash and raw evidence. Exact native efficiency changes should have fixed-traversal agreement where their contract permits; selective coverage changes need independent tactical/endgame truth and practical equal-time results. Run the combined candidate across the whole frozen development collection and once across untouched held-out families at the coherent checkpoint. Record exact improvements, exact regressions, published-preference changes and unresolved reference judgments separately; do not add them into one accuracy number. Unlisted positional alternatives require independent continuation adjudication, not automatic failure.

If a held-out case is inspected to motivate a fix, promote its whole related family into development and replace it independently; report first-pass validation separately from later contaminated checks. If the coherent round gains only throughput or motivating-example success without broad decision benefits, say so and reconsider the hypothesis. Do not launch a tournament or claim a strength increase from the benchmark alone.
