# MSH-155 time-scaling diagnostic: independent prospective methodology review

Reviewer read repository AGENTS.md, Backlog instructions overview, design history/search boundaries, MSH-154 assessment, methodology and verification records. Observed repository source dcc17e1. No engines were run and no repository files were edited. This review precedes new answers; prior failures and successes are necessarily known from the earlier assessment.

## Disposition

Proceed with the proposed small matched-time panel subject to the controls below. It can establish selected-position decision behavior and how that behavior changes with thinking time. It cannot establish relative playing strength, Elo, broad tactical error prevalence, or that a speed optimization will reproduce a longer-time decision. Call the cross-engine comparison a **matched-time decision-quality comparison**. If reporting competitiveness, qualify every claim as competitiveness on this selected diagnostic panel. Published opponent ratings do not calibrate its score.

Eight roots, two in each tactical/positional/defensive/endgame stratum, provide coverage rather than representative sampling. Four fresh roots plus two historical failures and two historical successful controls are defensible. Preserve the fresh/historical split in every summary; historical cases are enriched for known time-sensitive behavior. Four fresh roots are confirmation examples, not an independent population estimate. Freeze root identities, histories, stratum and outcome rubric before seeing any of the three current engines' answers. Prefer one fresh root per category; avoid duplicate games or nearly identical mechanisms across the fresh set. Do not replace hard or ambiguous roots after results arrive. Record overlaps when a root fits multiple categories.

## Outcomes and admissible comparisons

For each root predeclare its question: forced material consequence, tactical resource, defensive survival, conversion/draw preservation, or a specific positional continuation. Provide source and provenance, expected acceptable move/outcome set where independently justified, critical best-defense continuation branches, known alternatives and an uncertainty label. A tactical answer should be supported by replayed replies and endpoints rather than the puzzle's one advertised move. Quiet positions often have multiple good moves; exact top-move agreement is then an invalid metric. An unlisted move is unclassified until reviewed, not automatically wrong.

Use outcome labels such as supported acceptable, supported deterioration, unresolved, and invalid run. Keep a separate evidence tier (proved/independently established; finite-reference supported; qualitative provisional). Do not average these tiers into a centipawn-loss or win-rate proxy. Improvement means a supported transition from worse to better outcome, including an independently supported continuum within a category where meaningful; a move change, score increase, deeper iteration, or longer PV alone is not improvement. Stable acceptable moves count as stability, not failure to improve. More time can regress: record all reversals and mixed trajectories.

A small independent stronger reference is a fallible adjudicator, not an oracle. Goldfish and Oxidation are comparison subjects and must not define their own correctness. Agreement of all three is suggestive but not proof. Preserve uncertain reference opinions, bounds and conflicting plausible alternatives as unresolved. Do not spend the entire reference budget proving a single preferred explanation. Preallocate adjudication to uncertain alternatives that could change the stated conclusion; a 100-second allowance may be insufficient for all eight roots and all distinct moves. In that case report partial adjudication, without inventing binary labels or escalating compute implicitly.

Compare each engine against itself across 1/5/15 seconds for absolute decision improvement. Separately compare all three at each common time on roots that have adequate outcome evidence. Report root-by-root better/equivalent/worse/unresolved with the valid denominator, alongside outcome-tier information. Do not compare mShogi at 15 seconds with opponents at one second and call that relative scaling. A gap that narrows on two roots is a local observation; an unchanged small panel does not establish a ceiling or absence of general improvement. Cross-engine NPS, nominal depth, score units and WDL estimates are not comparable strength units.

## Execution controls

Use release frozen source, executable hashes and explicit configurations for all engines, one thread, no pondering, fresh process or verified new-game/cleared hash per independent root/budget. Record effective hash capacity rather than suggesting equal MB means equal internal opportunity. Use production mShogi search settings. Preserve complete history for historical positions, including counters and claims; fresh FEN-only states must explicitly declare unknown history and must not be used to draw history-sensitive conclusions. Verify initial state and legal full move prefix independently before search.

Use the same nominal thinking time with actual search elapsed and externally observed wall time recorded. Bounded stop/kill grace belongs in the ledger. Retain budget overrun, early stop, crash, invalid bestmove and incomplete information as separate failures, not chess outcomes. Run sequentially without other search workloads. Freeze a simple balanced engine order across roots/budgets or a seeded permutation to reduce systematic warmup/thermal order effects. Keep TT cold across time budgets; do not let the 15-second search inherit the one/five-second tree. Depth and node counts remain engine-specific diagnostics.

For every UCI score-bearing event preserve its root, move/PV, depth, score kind/perspective, lowerbound/upperbound flags and multipv index atomically, plus raw UCI. Do not merge the last score from one event with the depth or PV of another. Retain final bestmove separately: the last non-bound event may correspond to another move or earlier iteration and is not automatically the final move's exact value. A reported non-bound finite score is still not chess truth. Replay every full retained PV legally from the correct full-history root; short or legal PVs do not demonstrate that endpoint consequences were adequately searched or valued. Failed reference runs cannot supply partial chess evidence.

If independent reference adjudication is needed, prefer same-parent constrained candidates with identical nominal budget/history/settings; inspect best-defense continuations and resolved endpoints. Record differing completed depths and root branch allocations. Root-versus-child score subtraction combines different horizons and is not a precise loss metric. No numerical loss is inferred from lower/upper bounds treated as exact values. Merely seeing a defense in a PV does not localize a failure to evaluation, horizon or selectivity.

At most 100 seconds of independent reference adjudication leaves 116 seconds within the proposed 900-second aggregate ceiling for failures, stop grace and tightly chosen diagnostic probes. The ceiling is operational, not a target to exhaust. Freeze the 30-second escalation rule before baseline answers. Eligible roots must have a meaningful unresolved outcome disagreement at 15 seconds or a supported nonmonotone/time-sensitive decision whose 30-second result could change the next diagnostic priority. Rank by predefined evidence quality, then decision relevance, then stable root ID as tie-breaker; do not choose solely because mShogi loses or improves. Include at most one root for the same mechanism when alternatives exist. Record all eligible roots and why the selected ones outrank the remainder. Run all three at 30 seconds on each selected root even if the first result looks conclusive. Interpret this as adaptive selected follow-up, not the eight-root 30-second curve. If no informative unresolved root exists, omit escalation.

Stop after the frozen baseline and justified selected follow-up, or earlier on the operational ceiling, invalid harness behavior, or inability to adjudicate the declared question. Reserve enough ceiling before starting each batch to finish every matched cell and bound shutdown; otherwise leave the batch unrun. Do not spend the residual allowance on open-ended parameter sweeps. Additional runs must name the competing explanations they distinguish and fit the same ceiling. If timing variation can plausibly flip a key conclusion, a small repeat may be justified from reserve; repeated results remain observations on the same root, not independent samples.
