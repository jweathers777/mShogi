## 3. National Archives: the 1696 printing

### Copy identity and public interface

The [exact catalogue](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/file/3144172), basic and “all information” views, confirms **諸象戯図式**, author **西沢貞陣**, call **199-0411**, **元禄9刊**, **4冊**, former owner **昌平坂学問所**, collection **内閣文庫 → 和書 → 和書(多聞櫓文書を除く)**. “公開” is the access category; it does not mean digitized. The all-information view adds Japanese language and description level `file`.

No viewer/download control or linked manifest is exposed. The subordinate-list message explicitly says there are no subordinate items. Consequently:

- Complete four-book digitization: **not established**; four physical books are established.
- Public IIIF, image API, PDF or page JPEGs for this holding: **none discovered through the record's public links**. No hidden API paths or guessed image identifiers were probed.
- Tenjiku volume/folio range: **unverified**. The older handoff's proposed “first volume” is not an inspected locator and should not be used on an order as fact.
- Tenjiku setup, moves, promotions, explanatory clauses, title/preface and colophon: **not acquired**. Request their locations before specifying a paid range.

The [NIJL work record3048520](https://kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/work/3048520?ln=ja) independently lists four volumes/four books, 1696 printing, and an **内閣** printed holding. It is a work-level bibliography, not an additional facsimile or a second copy. The archive web reader failed to fetch the catalogue; the normal browser succeeded. This is not an authentication failure.

### Reuse and reproduction

The live [digital-archive reuse policy](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/secondary-use) permits copying/modifying/redistributing its supplied digital content without an application; it recommends source credit and disclosing alterations, with third-party rights remaining the user's responsibility. **CC0 is specifically the catalogue metadata designation.** These provisions do not establish that absent Tenjiku images exist, nor automatically license a future commissioned delivery. Confirm any accompanying conditions. Historical authorship and reproduction access/conditions remain separate questions; no new legal determination is claimed.

The official [remote-copy procedure](https://www.archives.go.jp/guide/copy.html) offers paid copies without a visit. Contact **業務課利用係, naj-riyou@archives.go.jp**, phone **+81-3-3214-0663** (fee sheet), fax **+81-3-3212-8807**. Use the linked 利用請求書 for copies or 特別複写申込書 for special photography, after staff identifies the suitable method and exact scope. Email applications accept PDF/Word/image attachments. Published delivery is paper or DVD-R, collected or shipped; email/download delivery and overseas shipping/payment require confirmation. Bank transfer or payment at the reading room is documented; allow roughly a month for open material, subject to condition/quantity.

The [current fee sheet](https://www.archives.go.jp/guide/pdf/kouhu.pdf), visually inspected in full, distinguishes these categories:

## 4. Kyoto manuscript

### Identity, dating and current catalogue

The [Doshisha institutional reference response1000247371](https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000247371&page=ref_view) identifies **象戯図式, 和/780/64, material119040343, bib305666** at **京都府立京都学・歴彩館**. It distinguishes material1119040370, another similarly titled item. It reports that Kyoto staff matched Masukawa's **Wa-Shogi** figures to **7丁裏–9丁表**. That is **not a Tenjiku range**.

The [current integrated catalogue's exact record](https://usaco.hosted.exlibrisgroup.com/primo-explore/fulldisplay?docid=kyotoshiryokan_limedio_book305666_100534972&context=L&vid=kyotoshiryokan&lang=ja_JP), reached through the institution's linked search, freshly confirms **象戯圖式**, **[書写者不明]**, **1冊,24cm,日本語**, holding **歴：貴重書庫F**, shelfmark **和||780||64||/541805**. Its `0000` publication/printing fields signify unknown placeholders, not year zero. Its physical availability indication is not scan availability. It links [current OPAC book305666](https://opacs.pref.kyoto.lg.jp/opac/book/305666); no image/IIIF/PDF link is exposed. Generic book icons in search results are not facsimiles.

The front-leaf acquisition note, reported by Doshisha, records purchase **25September1903** and names Matsuura. Copyist and copying date are unknown. Neither “1903 manuscript” nor “1909 manuscript” is an established production date. The JCSA bibliography describes a same-title work as a copy of 諸象戯図式, but that is not a copy-specific textual collation. **Independence of this witness, its relationship to1696/Harvard, and identity with Banaschak's source remain unproved.**

### Actual access obstacle and content limits

The dedicated OPAC (old link and current Riflis entry) and [Kyoto historical archive](https://www.archives.kyoto.jp/) returned explicit Cloudflare blocked pages in the normal browser. Public integrated-catalogue access succeeded by the institution's separately linked discovery service; no restriction was bypassed. The archive search therefore could not be completed in this environment. Web-reader failures/403s are logged separately from successful indexed guidance reads.

**Digitization unknown; full-page images and IIIF not located; no historical pages acquired.** Library references strongly motivate checking this copy for Tenjiku, but the inspected catalogue does not enumerate its contents. A [Saitama Central Library response1000233564](https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000233564&page=ref_view) identifies Tenjiku diagrams reproduced from 象戯図式 in Masukawa's *Shogi*, pp110–111 (its cited edition1985). Connecting those figures to Kyoto's exact manuscript is a plausible lead, not an authenticated page match. Ask staff to identify the Tenjiku heading and surrounding leaves.

### Procedure, costs and conditions

Kyoto's [rare/special-material use guidance](https://www.pref.kyoto.jp/rekisaikan/eturan1.html) requires prior arrangement and permits staff to refuse or specify reproduction methods. The relevant contact is **資料課, rekisaikan-shiryo@pref.kyoto.lg.jp**, rare/books desk **+81-75-723-4833**, fax **+81-75-791-9466**, **〒606-0823 京都市左京区下鴨半木町1-29**.

The [postal-copy page](https://www.pref.kyoto.jp/rekisaikan/copyservice.html) accepts preliminary inquiries even when pages are not yet identified. Its ordinary service covers books/journals, excludes old-document/admin-document categories, and requires prepayment plus postage by bank transfer or registered cash. **A rare manuscript's eligibility must be confirmed**, even though this copy is catalogued in rare-book storage. No overseas delivery or emailed-scan promise was found.

The [official form download page](https://apply.e-tumo.jp/pref-kyoto-d/downloadForm/downloadFormList_detail?tempSeq=128) supplies the current form/rates: counter copies ¥20 B&W/¥30colour; photography ¥110 basic +¥50/frame, CD-R writing ¥350 or DVD-R ¥600. For10photographic frames, that suggests ¥960 with CD or ¥1,210 with DVD, **only if staff confirms applicability**, plus shipping/payment charges. The form restricts copies to research and asks for a separate publication notice for publication use. Public archive content is generally [CC BY2.1JP, with exceptions](https://www.pref.kyoto.jp/rekisaikan/usage.html); that does not automatically govern an unpublished or specially supplied copy.

## 6. Adams/Banaschak, Muller and the unresolved questions

Adams's [second-edition PDF](http://history.chess.free.fr/papers/Tenjiku-Colin%20Adams.pdf), Appendix A printed166–168/PDF177–179, attributes the translation and footnotes to **Peter Banaschak**, except Adams-labelled comments. It names an **undated Shōgi zushiki**, warns of title ambiguity, and gives no institution/call number. Relative pages1–5 describe dimensions, setup, promotions and movement. They cannot be mapped to199-0411, Kyoto folios or Harvard without comparing images. A.2 mentions Maruo Manabu's Kyoto group and *Kapitan*, without identifying an issue or archive copy. The earlier handoff's spelling “Kiapitan” is corrected here without rewriting that dated record.

The [earlier dispute ledger](tenjiku.md) remains useful. Obtaining Japanese diagrams and text could test Fire Demon's ray orientation, Lion Hawk, generals' “flying over,” Tetrarch, Free Eagle and multi-step terminology. It could also test whether burn order/immunity/mutual destruction and promotion effects have explicit clauses. Royal, repetition and ending rules require surrounding general-game text; movement diagrams alone cannot supply them. **No historical dispute is resolved here.** The possible value of a page is distinct from a promise that it will answer a modern sequencing question. Muller's modern burn/movement conventions do not identify a historical copy.
