# MSH-159 (Classical Evaluation Decisions)

**Completed investigation: reject the experimental candidate; retain the verified predecessor.** Candidate `96e4bcd6307409e988ffde1c8862aecfc7af39e3` corrected independently supported evaluation weaknesses and improved the two motivating five-second choices. Wider testing also found two material decision regressions. Independent review therefore rejected the whole frozen candidate under its recorded retention rule. ## Established weaknesses and their limits

The complete evaluator review covered untapered material; tapered placement/centrality; pawn structure, advancement and king escort; geometric mobility and threats; major-piece king pressure/conversion; and specialized low-material overrides. The ordinary endgame terms rewarded advanced passers strongly while representing current bishop restriction weakly. King centrality did not itself represent useful flank routes; that observation motivated diagnosis but did not justify a generic king-distance bonus. Specialized KPK and native outcomes retain their existing precedence. See [system review](evidence/msh159/evaluator-review.md) and [diagnosis](evidence/msh159/eval-diagnosis.md).

For 0UOyM, independently verified tablebase endpoints exposed a concrete static sign/ranking error: from White's perspective, a losing race endpoint scored **+55**, while an alternative winning endpoint scored **+43**. The candidate scores them **−245/+343**, respectively. The correction represents promotion timing, including an extra preparatory turn when the runner's own king obstructs its file and no current capture bypass exists. These are board-level endpoint results, not root-history WDL. Independent full-history finite analysis supports exf3+ over Kg6 and Kh6 over g6, including stronger defenses than the published continuation.

For 0m5mI, the matched after-e3 static comparison preferred Kc2(+14 White) over Bc1(+12). Independent three-second forced-move analysis supports Bc1, while showing why current loose-pawn pressure and subsequent Bd2 promotion control matter. Such finite analysis is supporting evidence, not exhaustive minimax proof. Bc1 does **not** control e1 after ...e2; the implementation assigns no future Bd2 credit. The predecessor already preferred Bd2 to Kd2 once that move was available, so the defect was not universal failure to recognize bishop blockade.

## Experiments and candidate mechanisms

The initial opposite-bishop discount produced Kc3 but had a real guard defect: geometric king attack on a protected bishop incorrectly removed its restriction, even when capture was illegal. That result is an invalid experimental artifact, not evidence for retaining the original formula. Correcting this guard alone left the root on Bd6. Independent review also rejected discounting a protected intermediate landing merely because a bishop attacks it: the pawn can advance under cover and capture would lose the bishop. Both defects have native legality, positive/negative and reflected regression controls. Trial receipts and failures remain preserved.

The final candidate combines two general features in disjoint material regimes:

- **Pawn-only race forecast:** one capped300 endgame premium per side. It requires a strict passer, clear runway, current pawn-attack and generous king-interception checks, and at least a three-ply lead over the fastest optimistic estimate of every opposing pawn. A guarded extra preparatory turn accounts for an own king ahead on the file; clearing that king or creating a later capture bypass costs a turn. This is not a complete promotion scheduler or proof of conversion after queening.
- **Opposite-bishop passer restriction:** exactly one bishop per side, opposite colors, no knights or major pieces, and an advanced strict passer. A secure opposing bishop must physically occupy the front, control promotion, control an undefended intermediate landing, or attack the currently undefended pawn. Nonking attack on the bishop releases the guard; nearby enemy king approach does so unless king/pawn defense protects the bishop. Only advancement premiums are suppressed, retaining material, placement, base passer value and escort. No draw recognition or permanent-blockade claim is introduced.

No blanket opposite-bishop scaling, arbitrary score matching, general king-route bonus or unrelated valuation work was retained. See [race review](evidence/msh159/race-source-review.md) and [final source review](evidence/msh159/final-source-review.md).
