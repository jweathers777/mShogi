## Numerical summaries and uncertainty

Use native outcomes only. Verify exactly 100 color pairs: 90 prepared master-supported pairs and ten unaided pairs. Preserve source histories, family/structural metadata, opening/color and all claim/dead-position distinctions. Keep Mac, qualification, pilots and abandoned transition evidence separate. Report W/D/L and score fractions, without human-rating or Elo conversion or assuming the reference is demonstrably stronger.

For prepared score uncertainty, resample whole opening pairs with replacement within each frozen source family, preserving each family's observed pair count. Use 50,000 replicates, Python `random.Random(16120261113)`, and percentile .025/.975 endpoints. Sort frozen family names lexicographically and pair IDs lexicographically within family. Use one RNG, with replicate outer loop, then sorted family loop, then exactly that family's pair-count draws using rng.randrange(n). Sort the 50,000 replicate scores. For q=.025/.975 use index (B-1)*q, floor i, fractional h, and endpoint sorted[i]*(1-h)+sorted[i+1]*h (last index unchanged). Record Python version, exact input hashes, ordering and implementation hash. Singleton families, if present, contribute no within-family resampling variation; the result does not measure uncertainty in family selection. Each pair contributes the average of its two candidate game scores (win 1, draw .5, loss 0). These conditional sample intervals retain within-pair correlation; purposive master coverage and cross-source dependence still limit population inference. Report leave-one-family-out score sensitivity and per-family/color results. Unaided repeats are descriptive and separate; their repeated starts/trajectories do not become 20 independent population observations. Record unique complete trajectories and relevant repeated prefixes rather than pooling them into the prepared interval.

Use the frozen phase helper on complete native histories, with supplied moves context-only. Report the original opening boundary and material 26 convention. As descriptive sensitivity, shift resolved opening boundaries by ±2 plies and compare endgame material cutoffs 24/28 against 26; retain unresolved boundaries and promotion-driven phase changes. This is sensitivity analysis, not a change to rules or query admission.

Reconcile every played decision of both engines to one exact native identity and full-ledger disposition, including screening-only and unselected roots. Record evidence tier (500k, matched 5M, selected 20M or admitted mixed expansion), original flags and the exact final overlay where queried. Preserve supplied prefixes separately as context. Assert complete, unique played-root coverage against all native histories; never upgrade screening-only or unselected uncertainty to a confirmed non-error. Report total played/evidence-tier counts separately from selected-root denominators and coded supported-event counts. Reconcile every selected root to its exact final ledger and original native identity. Preserve supported finite, subthreshold, same-choice, unresolved and claim-limited/underlying statuses separately. Compare support at .04/.05/.06 using each source's retained stability/bound/range evidence, including stable threshold-straddlers where appropriate. Do not silently equate a selected-score difference with a stable conservative margin or turn unresolved roots into non-errors. If source schemas differ, document normalization and independently check it. Secondary support is separate from fresh primary comparisons; absent old normalized tags is not proof of primary causation.

## Behavioral coding, episodes and ranking

Code observed decisions from the completed independent narratives, exact native boards and legal matched alternatives. Use the main method's candidate categories: opening/development, king safety, tactical calculation, quiet defense, coordination/activity, pawn structures/breaks, planning, exchanges, opportunity response, advantage preservation/conversion, and endgame decisions. Preserve explicit unknown/uncertain coding instead of forcing a mechanism. Multiple tags may describe one root, so category totals can overlap. Distinguish decision behavior, context and possible mechanism; the occurrence of a capture, pawn move or endgame alone does not establish its defect.

Report category affected games AND moves, with severity, phase, family, color and actual result, plus reviewed episodes linking opponent contributions and candidate responses. Do not sum correlated margins or count adjacent reversals as independent causes. Favorable episodes require the frozen stable E criteria; actual results and claim censoring remain separate from finite advantage. Count only defensible coded episodes; disclose incomplete/uncertain boundaries and overlapping themes. Preserve successes and withdrawn 5M concerns as contrary evidence.

Rank recurring supported behavior using affected-game breadth, consequential episodes, severity and cross-family/phase/result evidence separately, with leave-one-family and unresolved-case sensitivity. Do not fabricate a universal frequency×severity metric. Category counts describe observed/coded sample evidence, not an unbiased all-Chess error rate. Unresolved-case sensitivity must distinguish a conservative supported account from plausible additional unresolved cases; no invented labels, probabilities or upper-bound exactness. No first/largest cause unless all relevant earlier uncertainty permits it.
