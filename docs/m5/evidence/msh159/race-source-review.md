# MSH-159: independent pawn-race source review

Verdict: no source-level blocker to trial2 or progression to wider development validation. Production retention remains contingent on outcome/control evidence and final frozen-source checks. Reviewed /tmp/msh159/trial2-built-eval.rs against trial1-baseline-eval.rs. SHA256 ba892c7ac8349c296a8c8c2dc94f457c2d48fca080cf741c6479fa1a4edd6a95 matches metadata. Reviewer ran no engines, builds or tests and read no held-out data.

The isolated change adds a general pawn-only race feature with a300 endgame cap per side. It leaves material, native authority and search unchanged. Promotion plies use relative rank, side to move and optimistic initial double pushes. A mandatory two-ply clearance allowance is applied only when the pawn's own king occupies a square farther ahead on its file, the pawn is strictly passed, and no other friendly pawn lies farther forward on any file. The opponent's fastest estimate takes the minimum over every opponent pawn, including blocked pawns. The candidate receiving the bonus must itself be strictly passed, have every runway square empty and outside current enemy pawn attacks, and outrun a deliberately generous enemy-king interception calculation at every landing. A three-ply lead is necessary.

The clearance guard has a defensible general mechanism. A king cannot be captured, so a straight runner blocked by its own king needs an additional own turn. Strict passing alone is insufficient: a distant opposing pawn could capture another friendly pawn ahead and enter an adjacent file, offering a diagonal bypass. The no-friendly-pawn-ahead guard excludes that case. Moving a currently non-forward friendly pawn to create such a bridge itself costs an extra own turn, so does not remove the mandatory-tempo lower-bound rationale. No counterexample found under the stated pawn-only, pre-promotion assumptions. This should remain described as an optimistic race estimate, not a complete legal promotion scheduler or proof across arbitrary future promotions, checks and king obligations.

Multiple qualifying passers assign result[side]=300 rather than add; a single side cannot gain repeated premiums. The documented per-color conversion bound rises144→444; the other stated bounds sum below30000, so no static/mate-band collision is introduced. Endgame taper and specialized KPK override remain intact. Empty pawn lists do not overflow: the opponent-minimum sentinel uses saturating subtraction. Rank arithmetic is bounded by native validated pawn ranks and strict direction checks.

Tests independently exercise the observed tablebase-supported sign/ranking defect, explicit b4/a3/Black g4 promotion tempos, direct capture bypass, distant pawn bridge guard, color reflection, initial double push, side to move, blockers, enemy pawn control, king interception, non-pawn material exclusion, specialized KPK precedence and the one-bonus cap with multiple passers. The endpoint expectations are grounded in retained tablebase receipts, not desired engine moves. Earlier independent receipt hash checks confirmed cached endpoint White loss, alternative branch White win and capture-route state White loss. These are board-level outcomes; native repetition/claim history still governs actual game outcomes, and the cached TT continuation is not automatically the leaf that produced its bound.

Limits: even a safe current runway and a three-ply head start do not prove a won queen ending. Future checks, king duties and tactical use of the first queen remain outside this feature. The same bonus may occasionally favor the wrong route; wider existing development controls and final exact-outcome comparisons must assess that risk. The feature corrects a concrete static ranking and reportedly changes the five-second root choice to exf3+, but a move change alone is not final retention evidence. No bishop-blockade experiment is included in this source.
