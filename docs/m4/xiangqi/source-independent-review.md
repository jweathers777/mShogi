## Repetition: Chinese resolves a translation addition, not the complete software trigger

1. **English19.8 adds a number absent from Chinese19.8.** EN PDF44/printed36 calls perpetual check a threefold repetition of position. ZH PDF28/printed27 instead defines uninterrupted checking that develops a returning-to-original state, with no numerical count. Therefore claiming that both primary languages independently prescribe threefold is incorrect.
2. **Neutral four-times wording survives Chinese comparison.** EN3.2B PDF15/printed7 says four times, no illegal moves, neither player willing to change. ZH3.2B PDF10/printed9 likewise has a returning cycle, neither side offending, and persistence through four times. Chinese does not erase the four-times phrase. Whether the count means position occurrences including the initial state or completed returns/cycles needs an express software interpretation.
3. **The warning procedure is not merely a counter.** ZH3.1C PDF10/printed9 explicitly says three cycles, then referee requires change, then continued unchanged repetition loses. EN3.1C PDF14/printed6 describes three-fold prohibited repetition and warning/refusal. Arts3.1H/I and20 identify who owes a change. Arts20.1/3 speak of losing classifications, while Art3.1C supplies a warning/refusal procedure. Replacing this by immediate loss at an arbitrary occurrence deletes a material tournament step unless explicitly approved as software adaptation.
4. **Chapter5 does not settle exact universal timing.** ZH diagrams1/2 PDF31/printed30 both list six Red moves but omit Black's sixth reply. EN diagram1 PDF48/printed40 supplies Black's sixth reply, while EN diagram2 leaves it blank. Diagram1 cycles over two rounds. With Black's sixth reply it reaches four occurrences of the original position including the initial one; without that reply it does not. Both languages instruct the offender to change or lose, without a numbered warning event. Diagram3 also illustrates classification rather than providing a uniformly executed stop position. Thus a reconciliation of three completed cycles with four position occurrences is plausible, but cannot be reported as unambiguously proved for every event from these examples.

**Owner options that need precise proposal:** (A) preserve an explicit warning/change event, with a specified cycle recognition/initial-position count and refusal trigger; (B) explicitly identify a deterministic software adaptation that awards a result directly at a specified occurrence. Neither is an implementation choice until owner records it. Recommend a WXF2018-based, separately versioned software profile only after these semantics are approved. Do not quietly replace four-times neutral repetition with CXA threefold, or use English19.8 as authority for every cycle type.

## Natural move count: units resolved; claimant and event policy still material

- ZH3.2D PDF11/printed10 explicitly gives **50 rounds, 100 moves total** without any capture. EN3.2D spans PDF15–16/printed7–8; the English fifty-moves wording is less precise. ZH Art8 PDF16/printed15 again says both players together make100 steps without capture. This resolves 100 total plies, not100 full rounds.
- **Art8 Chinese check deduction is claimant-specific.** The limit is on the draw-claiming party's checks: up to10 count, excess are invalid steps and deducted. It does not say to subtract both sides' checks globally. EN PDF25/printed17 is more circumlocutory about the player requesting verification, but consistent with that narrower reading. A profile that globally deducts all excess checks has changed the primary rule.
- Art8 frames natural counting as an adopted tournament method; it starts at the first move and refers to no-capture counting. A capture-reset interpretation is sensible, but the inspected page is not a complete formal definition of every reset/subsequence boundary. Do not import pawn-progress resets from chess.
- The event matters. Art3.2D requires referee verification; Art8 allows player request or referee initiative. ZH Art8 gives a failed claim one technical foul and deducts five minutes, with time-exceeding leading to loss; PDF17 says inadequate reconstruction also treated as below100. EN continuation PDF26/printed18 was visually inspected on follow-up and confirms foul/five-minute penalty/arbiter initiative. Its failed-claim test says count did not exceed100, whereas Chinese says below100; this is another English boundary-wording inconsistency. Chinese3.2D/Art8 give100 as threshold; do not treat exact100 as a failed claim on that English phrase alone.
- Since Chinese speaks of **claimant** checks, referee-initiated verification has an unresolved claimant selection question for a deterministic autonomous software implementation. Also define result precedence if a board win and count threshold arise together; “regardless of position” is material source wording, not license to infer all event ordering.

**Recommendation for owner proposal:** retain side-indexed check counts and exact capture history, expose separate claim/referee or approved automatic-result policy, and label any removal of physical-event penalties as software adaptation. A fixed100-ply global counter alone is insufficient.

## Classifications and exceptions: substantive WXF scope

EN Articles19–20 PDF43–47/printed35–39 and ZH PDF27–30/printed26–29 were directly compared. The classification cannot be reduced to a Boolean check plus attacked-piece set.

- Check, mating threat, chase, exchange, block, offer and idle are distinct. Perpetual chase concerns continued pursuit of the **same victim**, including direct neutralization by another defending piece. Real/fake root concerns actual recapture capability, not geometric defense alone (19.10–15).
-20.1: unilateral perpetual check owes change/loses; mutual perpetual check draws.3.1H gives unilateral checking priority over opposing perpetual chase.
-20.2: long mating threats and listed mixtures such as alternate check/chase or check/idle draw if neither changes. A check/chase alternation is not automatically unilateral perpetual check or unilateral perpetual chase.
-20.3–5: one or multiple pursuers of the same target differ from pursuit distributed across multiple targets; an uncrossed enemy pawn exception and own king/pawn participation exceptions apply.20.5's “two-to-one chase” is a **frequency/counter-chase term** defined19.18, not simply “two pursuer pieces.” EN20.5 appears to misrender this as player using two pieces; Chinese20.5 instead says the side making two chases (on same victim). Do not encode a piece-count shortcut from that English sentence.
-20.6: a real root usually permits the cycle, fake root does not; horse/cannon perpetually pursuing a protected chariot remains prohibited.
-20.7: same-type pursuit normally permitted, but a target constrained to its line remains chase; a free horse pursuing an impeded horse is prohibited. Pin/leg constraints must survive into fixture expectations.
-20.8: a chase that also offers exchange does not automatically become harmless exchange.
-20.9: king/pawn pursuit and their combination with chariot/horse/cannon have exemptions.
-20.10: perpetual block/offer/exchange/threat to capture-after-check have their own draw categories.

The WXF20.2 enumerated list omits alternate mating-threat/chase although19.16 defines the term; however this is **resolved by WXF own diagram38**, EN PDF66/printed58 and ZH PDF49/printed48, both independently visually checked on follow-up. Black cannon alternates chase/mating threat without offense; Red chariot perpetually pursuing the unprotected cannon owes change. Thus no AXF graft is needed. Distinguish WXF diagram109, EN PDF106/printed98 and ZH PDF85/printed84, heading36.4: the same Red horse moves simultaneously threaten mate and chase an elephant; classify under heavier chase consequence, so Red owes change while Black elephant moves are idle. Alternating move categories and simultaneous dual classification require separate fixtures. AXF English PDF2–3 General3.2 also includes alternating mating-threat/chase, but ancestry alone would not have resolved this.

These are source coverage obligations, not independently verified algorithms. A comprehensive implementation must reconcile all applicable example families, overlapping labels, duty-to-change priority, and the warning procedure before claiming complete WXF conformance. No universal “protected pieces never chase” rule is supportable.

## Initial independently derived fixture expectations / counterexamples

These are planning fixtures, not executed tests. Human-readable coordinates use files a–i left-to-right from Red, ranks1–10 from Red's baseline. Coordinates are a temporary fixture convention, **not an adopted canonical record or protocol**.

1. **Same final board, opposite history classification:** board Red king d1, Red chariot e10, Black king f9, Red to move, all other intersections empty. Every listed move is a geometrically legal ordinary move; kings remain on palace intersections and never face. Each four-ply cycle returns identical pieces/side-to-move. First history consists entirely of Red checks; second has no check or material chase. Expect different classification/duty despite identical final board. The source supports unilateral checking responsibility versus neutral repetition; exact trigger/result remains an owner decision. Validate/replay against future rules implementation, never derive the expectation from it.
2. **WXF diagram1/2 transcription boundary:** import each official board and moves; retain a manifest flag for the absent Chinese Black sixth reply and EN diagram1's extra reply. Establish whether a reviewer-approved fixture stops before/after that reply. Expected classification: Red must vary or lose, not an unsupported immediate-loss ply. This catches counting an original position inconsistently and treating incomplete published histories as equivalent.
3. **Claimant-specific count ledger:** after100 no-capture plies, Red has given12 checks and Black0. Arithmetic expectation from Chinese Art8 is98 counted steps for Red claimant,100 for Black claimant (subject to the full legal history and event policy). A global counter returning one shared value fails the clause. This ledger is an arithmetic boundary fixture; constructing a fully replayed nonterminal history remains future fixture work.
4. **10/11 check boundary:** claims otherwise identical except claimant has10 versus11 checks: the latter requires one additional physical no-capture step to reach the same counted threshold. Opponent's11th check must not be silently deducted from claimant's count. Test capture boundaries and initial-history availability separately after policy is ratified.
5. **Root pairing:** same apparent attacked target/protector arrangement, one variant with legal immediate recapture and one with pinned/otherwise immobilized protector. Expected real-root versus fake-root classification; do not treat a pinned defender's geometric attack as protection. Also include horse/cannon versus genuinely protected chariot, which remains prohibited despite real root (20.6).
6. **Same-type and horse-leg pairing:** same-type mobile pursuit versus constrained target; free horse versus leg-blocked horse. Expected exemption in ordinary same-type case, duty to change in constrained case (20.7). Acquire board/move examples before acceptance; this is a required family, not a fabricated conformance pass.
7. **Mixed responsibility:** all-check versus alternating check/chase; all-check versus opposing perpetual chase; mutual all-check. Expect distinct20.1/20.2/3.1H classifications. Test overlapping simultaneous threats and two-to-one counter-chase (19.18/20.5); the latter must not be implemented as counting two attacking pieces.
8. **Target and actor identity pairing:** one same victim pursued through the cycle versus two alternating victims; own king/pawn involvement; uncrossed target pawn versus crossed target pawn. Expected exemptions/responsibility from20.3–5/9; persistent piece identity belongs in adjudication evidence, independent of board-key equality.

## Gaps and bounded review verdict

Ready to incorporate: numerical units (50rounds/100plies), absence of a numeric count in Chinese19.8, claimant-specific check deduction, material EN/ZH diagram stopping difference, and broad classification obligations. Still requires owner decision and/or acquisition: exact count/trigger, warning/refusal representation, autonomous natural-count verification policy, count/reset/result precedence formalization, full current AXF edition identity, CXA2020 authoritative fulltext/amendments, complete example reconciliation and independently approved legal fixtures. No profile can be called complete while these required semantics are merely marked unsupported.

No rule hybrid or program shortcut was adopted. Old AXF publications, WXF2018 and CXA2020 need distinct provenance; WXF's acknowledged ancestry cannot supply missing current clause identity. Comparative engines cannot decide the unresolved choices. This review may be used to challenge a proposal, but is not blanket approval of a future profile.
